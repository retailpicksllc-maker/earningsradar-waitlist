NVIDIA — Q4 FY25 Earnings Call
48-minute call · summarized in seconds
✦ AI Summary · Claude
Tone: Bullish Revenue $39.3B · +78% YoY EPS beat Guidance raised
TL;DR

Record quarter powered by relentless AI-infrastructure demand. Blackwell is ramping faster than expected, data-center revenue hit an all-time high, and management guided next quarter comfortably above Wall Street — supply, not demand, is the only limiter.

Key takeaways
  • Data-center revenue reached a record $35.6B, up ~93% YoY, on Hopper strength and early Blackwell shipments.
  • Blackwell ramp is ahead of schedule; management expects "several billion" in Blackwell revenue this quarter.
  • Gross margin held in the mid-70s and is expected to stay there as Blackwell scales.
  • New demand drivers called out: sovereign AI, enterprise agents, and inference workloads.
Guidance

Q1 revenue guided to ~$37B vs. ~$35.8B consensus; gross margin ~73–74% while Blackwell ramps, recovering toward the mid-70s later in the year.

Notable Q&A
Analyst: Is Blackwell still supply-constrained?
CFO: Demand continues to exceed supply; availability is improving each month, with a healthier balance expected in the second half.
Watch

Ongoing China export-control exposure and the pace of Blackwell margin recovery.
