Morgan Stanley (MS) Earnings Call Summary — Q2 FY26

Morgan Stanley is a major investment bank that now makes most of its money managing wealth for individuals.

Morgan Stanley (MS) reported Q2 FY26 results on July 15, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $3.46 against a $2.89 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line. Revenue of $21.35B landed above the $19.67B estimate. MS has beaten consensus EPS in 8 of its last 8 reported quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.
