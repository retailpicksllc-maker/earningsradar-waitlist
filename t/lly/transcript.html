Eli Lilly and Company (LLY) Earnings Call Summary — Q2 FY26

Eli Lilly is a drugmaker best known for weight-loss and diabetes blockbusters Zepbound and Mounjaro, plus cancer and Alzheimer's medicines.

Eli Lilly and Company (LLY) reported Q2 FY26 results on August 5, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $8.38 against a $6.40 consensus; the two figures are reported on different bases, so no beat or miss is claimed. Revenue of $22.97B landed above the $20.82B estimate. LLY has beaten consensus EPS in 6 of its last 6 scored quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.

Key highlights from the call

  • Operator: “Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for standing by, and welcome to the Lilly Q2 2026 Earnings Conference Call.”
  • Chief Scientific and Product Officer: “With the recent completion of TRIUMPH-2 and TRIUMPH-3 trials, we now have the clinical data package to support global registrations in obesity, obstructive sleep apnea, and knee osteoarthritis pain.”
  • President, Lilly USA and Global Customer Capabilities: “We continue to see significant growth even in Q2 in overall TRx coming into treatment of obesity on 3 million TRx and a lot of that is driven by both injectable and oral on new patients coming in.”
  • Analyst (Goldman Sachs): “Just curious as to what you think the contributing factors are there and when you think we could expect an inflection?”
  • President, Lilly USA and Global Customer Capabilities: “We're seeing week-to-week growth in overall prescriptions and uptake for Foundayo.”

What moved the stock

LLY reported Aug 5. The highlights: EPS of $7.95 — a big beat vs the $6.40 estimate (+24%); revenue of $22.97B — a beat vs the $20.82B consensus (+10%). Focus now shifts to GLP-1 (Zepbound/Mounjaro) supply and demand trends.
