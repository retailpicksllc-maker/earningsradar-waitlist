Walmart Inc. (WMT) Earnings Call Summary — Q3 FY26

Walmart is the world's largest retailer — thousands of superstores plus a fast-growing online and grocery delivery business.

Walmart Inc. (WMT) reported Q3 FY26 results on August 20, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $0.81 against a $0.74 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line. Revenue of $177.40B landed below the $186.62B estimate. WMT has beaten consensus EPS in 7 of its last 8 reported quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.

Key highlights from the call

  • CEO: “Back to the quarter, the team delivered strong sales and profit growth across each of our segments.”
  • CFO: “Our results were better than expected on the top and bottom line and reflect the advantages of our omnichannel model and the diversified nature of our profit streams.”
  • Analyst, Morgan Stanley: “The question is, do you think agentic AI will supercharge Walmart's e-commerce growth?”
  • CEO: “And I think when we put all those things together, it will be a very important growth aspect or channel for us.”
  • President and CEO, Walmart International: “And what we're seeing is that it's really attractive, and it's become up to about 20% of our e-commerce business in Chile already.”

What moved the stock

Highlights from WMT's Aug 20 report: EPS of $0.81 — a beat vs the $0.74 estimate (+9%); revenue of $177.40B — a miss vs the $186.62B consensus (-5%). Focus now shifts to e-commerce growth, grocery share gains, and the consumer read.
