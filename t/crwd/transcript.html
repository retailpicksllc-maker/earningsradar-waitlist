CrowdStrike Holdings, Inc. (CRWD) Earnings Call Summary — Q3 FY26

CrowdStrike sells cybersecurity software that protects companies' computers and networks from hackers.

CrowdStrike Holdings, Inc. (CRWD) reported Q3 FY26 results on August 26, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $0.31 against a $0.29 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line. Revenue of $1.47B landed above the $1.44B estimate. CRWD has beaten consensus EPS in 6 of its last 7 scored quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.
