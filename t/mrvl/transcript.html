Marvell Technology, Inc. (MRVL) Earnings Call Summary — Q3 FY26

Marvell designs chips for data centers and networking, including custom AI chips for cloud giants.

Marvell Technology, Inc. (MRVL) reported Q3 FY26 results on August 27, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $0.94 against a $0.93 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line. Revenue of $2.74B landed above the $2.72B estimate. MRVL has beaten consensus EPS in 7 of its last 8 reported quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.

Key highlights from the call

  • Chief Financial Officer: “Let me start with a summary of our financial results for the third quarter of fiscal 2026.”
  • Analyst: “If I run through those numbers, basically it sounds like you're implying somewhere around $10 billion in revenue for next year.”
  • Chairman and CEO: “I think you're absolutely in the ballpark when you add up the numbers I gave you on $10 billion for next year.”
  • Analyst: “Today, Matt, your lead AI customer announced their next-generation 3-nanometer AI XPU product.”
  • Chairman and CEO: “Nothing really new to report other than heads-down execution and strong product ramps coming in the time period I gave you, especially in fiscal '28 where you'll start seeing some of the 2-nanometer products ramp.”

What moved the stock

Marvell narrowly topped fiscal Q2 estimates, with EPS of $0.94 versus $0.93 expected and record revenue of $2.74B, up 37% year over year on AI demand. The stock still fell about 10% as investors focused on a softer gross-margin guide and questions around fiscal 2028 and the monetization timing of a major customer partnership.
