The Procter & Gamble Company (PG) Earnings Call Summary — June 2026 quarter

Procter & Gamble makes everyday brands like Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest and Charmin.

The Procter & Gamble Company (PG) reported its June 2026 quarter results on July 29, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $1.43 against a $1.41 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line. Revenue of $21.20B landed below the $21.38B estimate. PG has beaten consensus EPS in 7 of its last 8 scored quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.
