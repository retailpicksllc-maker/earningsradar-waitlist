The Procter & Gamble Company (PG) Earnings Call Summary — Q2 FY26

Procter & Gamble makes everyday brands like Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest and Charmin.

The Procter & Gamble Company (PG) reported Q2 FY26 results on July 29, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $1.43 against a $1.41 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line. Revenue of $21.20B landed below the $21.38B estimate. PG has beaten consensus EPS in 7 of its last 8 scored quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.

Key highlights from the call

  • Chief Financial Officer: “I will start with an overview of results for 2026, and Shailesh will discuss strategy, innovation, and focus areas as we start calendar year 2026.”
  • Chief Executive Officer: “Greater China Baby Care was one of the first categories to make a step change and continues to lead growth of the premium and super-premium segments of the market behind consumer insight-driven innovation and brand communication.”
  • Chief Financial Officer: “We expect stronger results in the second half which enables us to maintain fiscal year 2026 guidance ranges across organic sales, core EPS, and adjusted free cash flow productivity.”
  • Chief Executive Officer: “We continue to believe the best path to sustainable, balanced growth is to double down on the strategy: stronger integrated execution to delight consumers with superior products at a superior value.”
  • Chief Financial Officer: “If you look at Latin America, 8% growth; Europe in aggregate growing 3%; China growing 3% on top of 5% growth last quarter; Asia, Middle East, Africa is up 2%.”

What moved the stock

PG reported Jul 29. The highlights: EPS of $1.43 — in line with the $1.41 estimate; revenue of $21.20B — in line with the $21.38B consensus. Focus now shifts to volume growth and pricing normalization.
