Micron Technology, Inc. (MU) Earnings Call Summary — Q2 FY26

Micron makes memory chips (DRAM and flash storage), including the high-bandwidth memory that AI systems need.

Micron Technology, Inc. (MU) reported Q2 FY26 results on June 24, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $25.11 against a $20.98 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line. Revenue of $41.46B landed above the $35.91B estimate. MU has beaten consensus EPS in 8 of its last 8 reported quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.
