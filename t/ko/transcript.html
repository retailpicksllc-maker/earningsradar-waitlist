The Coca-Cola Company (KO) Earnings Call Summary — Q1 FY26

Coca-Cola sells the world's best-known soft drinks plus water, juice, coffee and sports drinks.

The Coca-Cola Company (KO) reported Q1 FY26 results on April 28, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $0.86 against a $0.81 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line. Revenue of $12.47B landed above the $12.24B estimate. KO has beaten consensus EPS in 6 of its last 8 scored quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.

Key highlights from the call

  • President and Chief Financial Officer: “Concentrate sales were 5 points ahead of unit case sales as the impact of six additional days in the quarter was partially offset by the timing of concentrate shipments.”
  • Analyst: “And on the volume front, just wondering is consistent unit case growth reasonable in the balance of the year with Easter help in Q1, some potential Ramadan impact?”
  • Chief Executive Officer: “More importantly, growing volume across all operating units, gaining share and also topping the EPS growth as well gives us confidence that we are on the right track.”
  • Analyst: “And I guess, as I think about the system broadly, I'd expect some of the pressures that we're all thinking about to be building a bit more acutely on your bottling partners already.”
  • Chief Executive Officer: “We had volume growth that gives us confidence not only at the core but across all the options and innovations we bring in different package sizes, which played a big role.”
