Tesla, Inc. (TSLA) Earnings Call Summary — Q2 FY26

Tesla makes electric cars (Model 3/Y/S/X, Cybertruck), batteries and solar, and is betting big on self-driving and robots.

Tesla, Inc. (TSLA) reported Q2 FY26 results on July 22, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $0.33 against a $0.50 consensus, so the company missed on the bottom line. Revenue of $28.24B landed above the $26.42B estimate. TSLA has beaten consensus EPS in 3 of its last 8 reported quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.

Key highlights from the call

  • CEO: “As we get approval for FSD in different countries, I think we'll see a similar uptick in demand.”
  • Chief Financial Officer: “So, Q2 continued the trend we saw at the end of Q1: a resurgence in demand for our vehicles across the globe.”
  • VP of AI: “We have driven more than 380,000 miles of unsupervised robotaxi now across six cities in two different states.”
  • VP of AI: “Regarding Optimus, especially Gen 3, Optimus has been designed to not just match the appearance of humans but also the functionality and dexterity of humans.”
  • Analyst: “What are you expecting to see and what would you like to see to make sure this is done properly?”

What moved the stock

TSLA reported Jul 22. The highlights: EPS of $0.33 — a sharp miss vs the $0.50 estimate (-34%); revenue of $28.24B — a beat vs the $26.42B consensus (+7%). Focus now shifts to deliveries outlook, margins, and robotaxi/FSD commentary.
