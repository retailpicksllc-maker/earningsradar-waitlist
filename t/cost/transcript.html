Costco Wholesale Corporation (COST) Earnings Call Summary — May 2026 quarter

Costco runs members-only warehouse stores selling bulk goods cheap; most of its profit comes from membership fees.

Costco Wholesale Corporation (COST) reported its May 2026 quarter results on May 28, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $4.94 against a $4.94 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line.
