Mastercard Incorporated (MA) Earnings Call Summary — Q2 FY26

Mastercard runs one of the two big global card payment networks, earning a small fee every time a card is swiped.

Mastercard Incorporated (MA) reported Q2 FY26 results on July 30, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $4.98 against a $4.77 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line. Revenue of $9.28B landed above the $9.08B estimate. MA has beaten consensus EPS in 8 of its last 8 reported quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.
