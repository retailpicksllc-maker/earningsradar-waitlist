Oracle Corporation (ORCL) Earnings Call Summary — Q2 FY26

Oracle sells database software and cloud services that big companies run their operations on, now a major AI data-center player.

Oracle Corporation (ORCL) reported Q2 FY26 results on June 10, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $1.79 against a $1.96 consensus, so the company missed on the bottom line. Revenue of $19.18B landed above the $19.10B estimate. ORCL has beaten consensus EPS in 4 of its last 8 reported quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.

Key highlights from the call

  • Chief Financial Officer (Principal Financial Officer): “As it relates to the numbers we are about to present, the following apply to both the results for Q2 and to our guidance for Q3.”
  • Executive Vice President, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI): “Our infrastructure business has grown at an accelerating 66% year over year.”
  • Chief Executive Officer: “As Doug shared, the total revenue growth of 13% in constant dollars — it's worth noting that's three consecutive quarters now where total revenue growth has been in double digits.”
  • Analyst, Deutsche Bank: “Congratulations and a particular shout-out to Mahesh and the team for standing up a massive amount of capacity in this quarter.”
  • Executive Vice President, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI): “The thing a lot of people don't understand is that we actually have many different options for delivering capacity to customers.”
