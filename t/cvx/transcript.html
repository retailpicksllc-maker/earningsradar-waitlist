Chevron Corporation (CVX) Earnings Call Summary — Q2 FY26

Chevron is a US oil and gas giant that explores, drills, refines and sells fuel around the world.

Chevron Corporation (CVX) reported Q2 FY26 results on July 31, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $6.06 against a $5.55 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line. Revenue of $70.06B landed above the $62.72B estimate. CVX has beaten consensus EPS in 7 of its last 8 scored quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.
