Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) Earnings Call Summary — Q2 FY26

AMD designs computer processors and AI chips — the main challenger to Intel in CPUs and Nvidia in GPUs.

Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) reported Q2 FY26 results on August 4, 2026. Earnings per share came in at $1.66 against a $1.62 consensus, so the company beat on the bottom line. Revenue of $11.54B landed above the $11.31B estimate. AMD has beaten consensus EPS in 7 of its last 8 reported quarters. Highlights from management's remarks on the call are below.

Key highlights from the call

  • Operator: “Greetings, and welcome to the AMD Second Quarter 2026 Conference Call.”
  • Executive Vice President, CFO and Treasurer: “I'll start with a review of our second quarter financial results and then provide our current outlook for the third quarter of fiscal 2026.”
  • Analyst, Barclays: “Lisa, you laid out the Analyst Day $1.4 trillion TAM on the accelerator side.”
  • Chair and CEO: “So look, we are certainly seeing a very, very strong compute market demand over both the data center accelerator as well as the server CPU.”
  • Analyst, Barclays: “But also on the server processor side, you would imagine that you're getting above corporate gross margins there.”

What moved the stock

Highlights from AMD's Aug 4 report: EPS of $1.66 — a beat vs the $1.62 estimate (+2%); revenue of $11.54B — a beat vs the $11.31B consensus (+2%). Focus now shifts to data-center GPU traction vs NVIDIA and PC chip demand.
